# Gift Concierge KG vocabularies

- `interests.json`: **31 interest labels**, version `1.1.0`
- `aspects.json`: **16 attribute aspects** (from listing metadata) and **12 quality aspects** (from reviews only), version `1.2.0`

This file is generated by `python -m knowledge_graph.vocab_docs` from the JSON vocabularies, which are the source of truth. Do not edit it by hand.

## Rules that apply to everything

- **Confidence values are heuristic rule scores, not calibrated probabilities.** They rank evidence tiers and nothing more. No precision has been measured yet; see `../eval/README.md`.
- **Aggregation: max.** An edge with several evidence items gets the highest item score. Scores are never combined (no noisy-OR). Every distinct item is kept in `evidence_list`, de-duplicated.
- **MATCHES is optional.** A product with no interest edge must remain eligible for recommendation. Use MATCHES to boost or explain, never to filter.
- **No recipient or occasion inference.** Recipients, occasions, age and budget belong in the conversation state.
- **No subjective quality from seller text.** Quality aspects have no listing rules; validation rejects any quality HAS_ASPECT edge that is not a review aggregate.
- **Not mapped on purpose:** `Religion & Spirituality > Occult & Paranormal`.

### Text matching

Whole words, case-insensitive; a space also matches a hyphen. A text match is dropped when it:

- overlaps an `exclude` phrase ("dog" in "hot dog");
- is negated in its clause ("not printed on wood");
- sits in an SEO keyword list of recipients/occasions, a long list of compatible uses, a run of five or more one- or two-word items ("Jewelry, Fishing Lures, Dolls, Hobby, RC"), or a compatibility clause ("Fit for TCG Yugioh card");
- is in a cross-sell sentence ("You Can Consider This Cat Puzzle");
- belongs to a product whose category path contains one of the concept's `suppress_text_in_categories`;
- belongs to a product whose title head names a suppressed product class:

  - `party_supplies`: Event supplies bought by a host (favors, invitations, toppers, balloons, decorations). A phrase introduced by "for"/"as" is a suggested use and does not count. Phrases: "party favor", "party favors", "party supplies", "party supply", "party decorations", "party decoration", "birthday decorations", "birthday decoration", "party invitations", "birthday invitations", "invitations", "cake topper", "cake toppers", "cupcake topper", "cupcake toppers", "balloon", "balloons", "balloon garland", "favor bags", "goodie bags", "treat bags", "party bags", "party pack", "tableware", "table toppers", "centerpieces", "party banner", "birthday banner".
  - `infant_nursery`: Nursery decor and infant products; motifs are chosen for a baby room, not a recipient interest. Phrases: "nursery", "newborn", "infant", "infants".

  The *title head* is the text before the first `,` `;` `|` `~` ` - ` or bracket, so SEO tails ("Beach Ball, Superhero Toys ... Pool Party Favors") do not change the product class. Category rules still apply to suppressed products. Books are never suppressed.

An interest supported only by feature phrases is counted as an *incidental mention* (`interest_incidental_mentions` flag) and creates no edge.

## Quality aspects (review evidence only)

Matched only in review text by `review_inference.py`, never in listing text.

**Per review** (`Review -SUPPORTS-> Aspect`): each matched clause is an evidence item with `evidence_polarity` positive, negative or neutral, its cue span, and confidence (direct 0.7, negated positive 0.6, neutral 0.5). The edge's `polarity` is positive or negative when all non-neutral items agree, `mixed` when they disagree, else neutral. The edge also stores rating, verified_purchase and helpful_vote.

- A negated positive cue ("not sturdy") is negative. A negated negative cue ("hasn't broken") is **neutral, not positive**.
- Hedged, counterfactual or questioning text ("hope it lasts", "looked like it would be informative", "is it durable?") is neutral.
- Negation and hedges are scoped to the clause up to the nearest comma before the cue. Sentences are split into clauses at contrast words (but, however, although, though, except, yet, unfortunately).
- `context_exclude`: an aspect is skipped in a clause mentioning one of its context words (durability is not judged from clauses about the box, packaging or shipping).
- **A star rating is never evidence on its own.** A positive item in a review rated <= 2 is flagged `rating_conflict` and does not count toward HAS_ASPECT.

**Per product** (`Product -HAS_ASPECT-> quality aspect`, source `review_aggregate`): created only when at least **2 distinct positive reviews** (identical texts count once; rating-conflict and mixed reviews excluded) support the aspect and positive reviews are at least **2.0x** the negative plus mixed reviews. The edge cites one positive clause per supporting review and stores all counts. **Negative evidence never becomes HAS_ASPECT**: an aspect with at least 2 negative reviews and more negatives than positives is recorded as a *concern* in the product's `review_aspect_signals`.

## Aligning with LLM constraint extraction

- The `label` values in `interests.json` are the shared interest IDs. The conversation component should restrict its `interests` output to these labels (e.g. a JSON-schema enum). The `aliases` give example user phrasings for its prompt.
- Specific franchises or terms (e.g. "Star Wars", "sourdough") go in a free-text field for BM25/semantic retrieval. They are not new KG labels.
- "Loves reading" is a domain preference (Books), not an interest.
- Both sides record the vocabulary version (`interests@1.1.0`). The label set has not changed since 1.0.0.

## Versioning

- **Patch**: phrase or exclusion fixes that do not change labels or edge semantics.
- **Minor**: added labels, or new matching policies or edge attributes that existing consumers can ignore (e.g. `match_type`, feature evidence becoming corroborating-only, review cues).
- **Major**: renamed or removed labels.
